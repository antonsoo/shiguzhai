# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-21

- Source-based exhibit of ten Shiguzhai bells: all 87 reported frequencies,
  museum identities, actual photographs and a bundled research paper.
- Listening by strike position, full B observation lists and a modern reference
  comparison; bounded Web Audio tones and PCM WAV export.
- Fixed frequency plot with an independently movable modern tuning grid,
  explicit grouping choices, and leading/all observation comparison.
- Three Chinese musical terms, with modern Mandarin readings and primary links.
- Shareable readings; standalone illustrated HTML with embedded photographs,
  source table and Chinese font; CSV and JSON exports; complete offline use.
