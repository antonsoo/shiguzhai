SYNTHESIZED SOUND AND NEW SCHEMATICS
Each two-second sound is generated from the selected two frequencies and a
modern, explicitly chosen decay envelope. It omits actual higher modes, impact
noise, room acoustics and measured damping. It is not a museum recording or a
reconstruction of ancient timbre. The lens-like rim and quadrupole drawings are
new teaching analogies, not measured or finite-element mode shapes.
The optional modern semitone grid is referenced to 364.07 Hz. It does not
establish ancient equal temperament or an A = 440 Hz reference.
